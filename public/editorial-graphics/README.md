# OPEN TAB editorial graphics kit

Branded SVG starters for consistent newsroom cards and social exports. Palette: near-black, white and signal red `#F04438`. Typeface fallback: Arial / sans-serif. Canvas: 1200 × 675 (16:9).

## Templates
- `/editorial-graphics/breaking-news.svg` — urgent, confirmed breaking-news updates.
- `/editorial-graphics/world-brief.svg` — international explainers and world desk roundups.
- `/editorial-graphics/entertainment.svg` — film, music, celebrity and creator coverage.
- `/editorial-graphics/sports.svg` — fixtures, results and sports explainers.

## Editorial rules
1. Replace placeholder headlines with short, factual copy and include the publication date where context requires it.
2. Do not imply a live emergency or confirmed breaking event unless an editor has verified it.
3. Do not use AI-generated or illustrative artwork as if it were a documentary photograph.
4. For photographs, verify reuse rights and add photographer/agency credit, original image source and licence/permission details.
5. Export social variants from the master 16:9 artwork only after checking platform crop-safe areas and legibility on mobile.
